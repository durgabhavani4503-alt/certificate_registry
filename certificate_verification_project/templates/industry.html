Industry Deployment Scenarios

Built for corporate onboarding teams, enterprise HR departments, and national registrar offices that need to stop credential fraud and retire slow, manual background-check email loops for good.

Corporate Onboarding Bureaus

Verify a new hire's degree the moment their offer letter goes out. One scan confirms the credential against the issuing institution — no waiting on a registrar's office to reply to an email.

Screen candidates at scale without opening a support ticket per applicant. Verification status is visible instantly, so hiring decisions aren't stalled behind paperwork.

The outcome: credential checks move from a multi-day email chain to a single scan — with a verifiable, fraud-resistant record behind every result.
